# 🩺 Health Journey

An AI-powered multilingual personal healthcare navigator that helps users organize, remember, and manage their healthcare journey without providing medical advice.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Health Journey is designed to simplify healthcare management by acting as a personal healthcare companion.

Instead of diagnosing or replacing doctors, it helps users:

- Track appointments
- Organize medical records
- Receive intelligent reminders
- Maintain healthcare history
- Navigate long-term health journeys

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Managing healthcare involves multiple appointments, prescriptions, laboratory tests, specialist visits, and follow-ups.

Many individuals and families struggle to organize this information efficiently, leading to missed appointments, forgotten tests, and fragmented medical history.

---

## 💡 Solution

Health Journey provides a centralized AI-assisted platform that helps users organize and manage their healthcare journey while encouraging consultation with qualified healthcare professionals.

---

## 🌍 Key Features

- AI Healthcare Timeline
- Smart Appointment Manager
- Medical History Tracker
- Multilingual Support
- AI Reminder System
- Health Document Organizer
