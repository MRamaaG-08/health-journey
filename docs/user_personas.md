# User Personas

## Primary Persona

### Busy Working Professional

Needs:
- Never miss appointments
- Organize reports
- Receive reminders
- Quick health overview

Pain Points:
- Busy schedule
- Scattered medical documents
- Forgotten follow-ups

---

## Secondary Persona

### Senior Citizen

Needs:
- Simple interface
- Large buttons
- Multilingual support
- Family assistance

Pain Points:
- Difficulty remembering appointments
- Paper-based medical records

---

## Supporting Persona

### Family Caregiver

Needs:
- Manage healthcare for parents or children
- Track multiple appointments
- Store family medical records

Pain Points:
- Managing multiple family members
- Keeping healthcare information organized
